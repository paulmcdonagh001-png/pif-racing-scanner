# PIF Racing — Actual historical market baseline (13 October 2026)

**Source:** Existing PIF_Racing_Real_Data_Pilot.xlsx derived from RacingFormBook 28 April–4 May 2025 sample.
**Data:** 269 races, 2,492 horses. **No Andy Holding speed-figure model has been backtested yet**: the available workbook does not contain adequate historical times, sectionals, or frozen pre-race speed predictions.

Method: Flat £1 stake per race on lowest industry starting-price favourite(s), equal divisions for joint favourites. Market SP known only at the off, so this is a *retrospective benchmark*, not actionable pre-race forecasting.
