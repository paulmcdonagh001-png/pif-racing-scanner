What we are testing

Can spotting horses that ran well in demanding races help us predict their next performances — and add anything the available odds did not already reflect? We record late progress, apparent race strength, jumping efficiency and changed conditions in our own words. We do not copy RaceiQ numerical sectionals into this notebook.
